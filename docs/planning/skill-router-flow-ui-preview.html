Privacy & Data

PKM is designed for local online weight tuning, not data collection or embedding-model training. Continual tuning is not enabled in the current release.

We do not collect queries

Personal Knowledge Manager does not collect, retain, or transmit your query text. Please do not submit or send us queries; we do not need them.

You choose detectionOnly rules you configure locally decide which interactions may create an aggregate tuning signal.
Queries stay outRaw query text is excluded from tuning records, logs, exports, and telemetry.
Local weights onlyTuning adjusts Router weights on this machine; it does not train an embedding model.

What PKM does

  • ✓Applies your local eligibility rules.
  • ✓Cleans and aggregates allowed outcome signals locally.
  • ✓Tunes Router ranking and fusion weights only.
  • ✓Visualizes local tuning progress and quality.

What PKM never does

  • ×Collect, store, upload, or ask you to submit query text.
  • ×Train, fine-tune, or modify embedding-model weights.
  • ×Send tuning records or learned weights to a remote service.
