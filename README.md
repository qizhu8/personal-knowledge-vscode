# Personal Knowledge Manager

**Your daily-work ecosystem, inside VS Code.**

Chat with AI, reuse your Knowledge, run repeatable work, and collaborate across
machines without scattering everything across disconnected tools.

[Explore Features & Screenshots](FEATURES.md) · [Installation](FEATURES.md#installation-guide) · [Changelog](CHANGELOG.md)

## More Than One Solution

PKM brings **Chat, Harness, Skills, Actions, Chatroom, Projects, Research,
utilities, Servers, and Sync** into one connected workflow.

| For Your Daily Work | What You Get |
|---|---|
| **PKM Chat & Harness** | Models, tools, pasted images, persistent Tasks, and visible execution. |
| **Knowledge & Research** | Reusable Skills, Notes, papers, links, and local search. |
| **Retrieval & Router** | Keyword search, optional local embeddings, and observable retrieval behavior. |
| **Actions & Projects** | Repeatable workflows, approvals, task context, and recovery evidence. |
| **Chatroom & Portal** | Work with people and agents, including Chats on other machines. |
| **Utilities & Servers** | Prompts, Scripts, Packages, Environments, MCP, and self-hosted services. |

## Across Platforms and Machines

Use PKM in VS Code on Windows, macOS, and Linux, including remote development
hosts. Carry reusable Knowledge between machines and connect specific Chats
through Portal with explicit permissions.

## Sync, Back Up, and Share on Your Terms

Use **GitHub Sync + backup** for continuity across machines and small-team
collaboration. Choose what to share and what stays private; manage repository
and sharing access yourself.

[Sync & Access Boundaries](FEATURES.md#github-sync-and-backup) · [Portal](FEATURES.md#portal)

## Get Started

1. Install **Personal Knowledge Manager** in VS Code.
2. Choose your Knowledge Root and follow the setup guide.
3. Open PKM Chat or add a Skill, Note, or Research item.

Local model retrieval is optional. Start with the built-in keyword search.
